## Archive evidence and request scope

- Use Drive revision history for authorship only for published meeting minutes.
  Bill and resolution credits come from archived introducer/author/sponsor lines
  or collective signatures; do not request their revision histories or spend AI
  generation calls on their credit labels.
- Prefer archived Bill Voting Records for explicit Senate passage status, with
  Senate minutes as the fallback when the register is blank or inconclusive.
  Committee recommendations and referral votes do not establish adoption.
  Preserve conflicting results as inconclusive rather than choosing one silently.
