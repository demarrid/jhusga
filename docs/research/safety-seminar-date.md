# Safety and rape-awareness seminar recommendation: contextual date

Reviewed 19 November 2026. Best estimate: **circa 1989, possibly 1989–90**.
This is an inference, not a date printed on the report. It dates the
recommendation, not a seminar proven to have taken place.

The source is [The Johns Hopkins University — The Student Council](https://www.jhusga.org/documents/2c260844-f5e7-4de2-b8fe-505abdd9c23b),
a five-image report beginning with IMG_2674. Its security section recommends
cosponsoring a safety/rape-awareness seminar. It is collectively signed by
Lou Giangiulio, Christian Reutter, and Jeffrey Perlstein.

Evidence and limits:

- A [15 February 1990 memorandum](https://www.jhusga.org/documents/daff5cc6-6c00-495d-8ebc-13b04485bec4)
  identifies Louis M. Giangiulio as Student Council president. JHU's indexed
  [1990 commencement program](https://jscholarship.library.jhu.edu/bitstream/handle/1774.2/36832/commencement1990.pdf?sequence=1)
  lists Louis Michael Giangiulio. Its [1991 program](https://jscholarship.library.jhu.edu/bitstream/handle/1774.2/36833/commencement1991.pdf?sequence=1)
  lists Jeffrey Mark Perlstein. These support the period but do not establish
  when the three signed this report. The commencement names were available in
  indexed search text; direct PDF retrieval returned HTTP 403.
- The report discusses National Issues Forums material on drug use and the
  environment. The [August 1989 NIF discussion guide, p. 40](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED317442.pdf)
  identifies drug crisis and environmental risk as two 1989–90 topics. However,
  its third topic is day care, whereas the Council report says crime: this is
  partial corroboration, not an exact match or a firm lower date bound.
- Its discussion of HOP programming and Major Larkin is consistent with
  [A Proposal for Quality Programming at Hopkins, 19 September 1989](https://www.jhusga.org/documents/d349f827-7576-4853-9d7e-ede1b33eb546),
  which discusses restructuring HOP and names Bob Larkin as Homewood security
  director. This is context, not proof the reports were written together.
- The report refers to security improvements made over summer and prospective
  presentation of a fee proposal to trustees in spring. This suggests an autumn
  report, but does not independently establish the year.

Use 1989 as a plotting year, accompanied by the explicit contextual-date note.
Do not assign a day or month, change the original archive text, or populate
Document.datedAt as if the report contained a date. The reviewed fallback in

or explicit event date takes precedence. Keep the existing event ID/citations
