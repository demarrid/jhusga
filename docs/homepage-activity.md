# Homepage evidence

Minutes credits use stored Google revision snapshots. Missing history permissions
allow a resolved owner fallback; other documents use their own named introducer,
author, sponsor, or collective sign-off without revision-history or AI calls.

Bills marked Passed use explicit Passed/Adopted rows in the archived Bill Voting
Records first. Senate minutes supply evidence when the register is blank, has no
matching file, or has contradictory results. Second-reading results take priority
over first-reading results. Committee recommendations do not count as passage.

Match files by Drive link or exact normalized title. Do not match by bill number
alone: the current register has inconsistent numbers across tabs. Undated
contradictory rows (such as CLeRPA's Adopted and Tabled entries) are inconclusive.
An undated adoption result does not invent a vote date for the card.

The archived voting workbook includes all four tabs. Existing first-tab-only
copies are re-exported even when Drive's modified time is unchanged. Status
questions in archive search prioritize this register over bill text.

Resolution highlights require verified passage, and all use the Resolution
label. The Inclusive JHU resolution is mapped to its approval in the November 19,
2024 Senate minutes, with the archived passage verified before it is included. The Class of 2025 thank-you
resolution stays in the archive but is excluded from homepage highlights.

A completed resolution enactment line with numerical passage votes also counts;
blank XX vote templates and committee recommendations do not. The Powers and
Authorities resolution records passage 23–0 with zero abstentions. Board of
Trustees stays off highlights until passage is verified.
